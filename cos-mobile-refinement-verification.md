# COS-Inspired Mobile Refinement Verification

The LESOUL home view retains a compact product-led entry experience and routes directly to product browsing, curated looks, and profile access. The catalogue continues to load its real product grid with new arrivals separated from the full collection.

The profile view now presents a compact summary using only real LESOUL data: saved looks, interested pieces, and purchase-list entries. It contains no delivery state, payment state, coupon, points, or order-status feature.

The five customer bottom-navigation destinations are Home, Browse Products, VIP Early Access, Curated Looks, and Purchase List. Non-VIP users retain the VIP gate rather than receiving product information.
