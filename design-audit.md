# COS Reference Comparison — 2026-08-28

## Reference patterns worth adapting

- A quiet, utility-led header: search, restrained brand mark, and one compact overflow control instead of several competing actions.
- A product-first two-column catalogue that starts close to the top of the viewport and gives imagery most of each card.
- A persistent, evenly spaced five-item bottom navigation with a clear selected state.
- A compact account overview: one identity card, then a short grid of actions; no shipping, payment, delivery status, coupons, or points are required for LESOUL.

## LESOUL strengths to retain

- Product cards already communicate stock, new arrivals, price policy, measurements, dark-colour notices, and purchase-list actions.
- Role separation, VIP early access, multilingual EN/KO/ZH labels, and administrator access are already established.
- The purchase-list-only workflow is appropriate because the project does not intend to take payment or arrange delivery in-app.

## Design direction

Adopt COS's information hierarchy and density rather than its branding or commerce operations. LESOUL should use a compact utility header, a closer-to-top two-column product grid, a five-item role-aware bottom navigation, and a concise profile overview based on saved looks, purchase list, VIP early access, and account access.
