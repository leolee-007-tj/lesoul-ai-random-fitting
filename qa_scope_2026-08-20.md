# Full App QA and Polish Scope

## Objective

Perform a customer-facing quality-assurance and visual-consistency pass without changing pricing, permission, product-data, AI-generation, fit-engine, purchase-list, curated-outfit, product-status, discount, or My Looks business rules unless a defect requires it.

## Required coverage

Review actual routes and primary NORMAL, VIP, and ADMIN flows across Login, Home, New Arrivals, catalog, product detail, Style This Item, AI Style Match, VIP AI, My Looks, Purchase List/Request, Product Manager, Outfit Management, user/AI operations, AI settings, and category management.

Test mobile-first at 390px, then 430px, 768px, 820px, and 1440px. Include Korean, Simplified Chinese, and English locale checks.

## Visual QA focus

- Remove overlapping buttons, badges, headers, bottom navigation, modal overflow, text clipping, and unsafe horizontal scroll.
- Normalize product-card image, one-line name, short information, size/price, status, and fixed-position CTA layout.
- Keep product prices and sales visually aligned; avoid image-badge congestion.
- Ensure Style This Item has no large direct-purchase CTA and uses compact purchase-list actions where applicable.
- Preserve quiet-luxury warm-white, beige, black, serif, and rounded-card design language while reducing excessive decoration.

## Functional QA focus

- Verify curated outfits are consistently used by Style This Item and AI Stylist.
- Verify locale-dependent dynamic content, My Looks deletion persistence, role navigation, AI ON/OFF behavior, Quick AI ADMIN-only policy, VIP model visibility, empty/loading/error states, and safe-area behavior.
- Prioritize fixes as P0 (app/data/permission blockers), P1 (overlap/deletion/data defects), P2 (translation/alignment/mobile), then P3 (spacing/typography/polish).

## Final report requirements

Report P0–P3 fixes, total found/fixed/remaining issues, key screens, mobile and locale coverage, role coverage, regression tests, changed files, and the stable rollback checkpoint.
